validated VIR. Each original public-body term is lowered under its exact source
receiver. SourceShape resolves to the independent representation-only count/domain
implementation, including canonical padding and cumulative cell bounds. Each

recursive public domain. Enum arms compare all underlying physical bits against
the declared value, including signed two's-complement and UInt64 values.
